Privacy Policy

Huebound · Oqelith Studios · Effective date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Huebound (the “Game”), published by Oqelith Studios (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play it on your Android device. The short version: Huebound does not collect, transmit or sell your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Huebound does not require an account, does not ask you to sign in, and does not send your data to our servers. The Game has no analytics, no advertising and no social features at the time of this policy.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue playing, the Game saves the following only on your own device:

  • Your progress: current level, best level, best score per level and achievements progress.
  • Your in-game items: coins, gems, power stock, unlocked themes and the theme you selected.
  • Your settings: sound and music on/off.
  • The player name you choose to type in the Game (this can be any name or nickname; please do not enter personal details).
  • The date of your last daily reward, so the daily reward timer works.

This data stays in the Game’s private storage on your device. We cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings.

3. Permissions

Huebound does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. Information handled by Google

If you download Huebound from Google Play, Google may collect certain information about your device and your use of the Play Store, and may provide us with aggregated, anonymous reports such as crash and performance statistics (for example through Android vitals). This is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your name, email address or other personal identifiers from these reports.

5. In-game currency and items

Coins and gems are virtual items earned by playing. They have no real-world monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money, and are stored only on your device. The Game does not currently offer purchases with real money.

6. Children’s privacy

Huebound is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the Game collects no personal information, there is nothing to delete or disclose about a child’s data.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with third parties, because we do not collect it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because all Game data is stored on your device, you control it. To delete it, uninstall the Game or go to Android Settings → Apps → Huebound → Storage → Clear data. Clearing the data resets your progress.

9. Security

Your Game data is kept in the app’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Since no data is sent to us, there is no server-side data for us to protect or lose.

10. Future changes to the Game

We may add features in later updates, such as sign-in with a Google account, cloud save, leaderboards or advertising. If a feature changes what information is collected or shared, we will update this policy and the Data safety information on the Google Play listing before or at the time that update is released, and we will change the effective date above.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to provide or remove, but you are welcome to contact us with any question or request.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
